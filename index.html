My Dearest

Love is hard.

You are not hard to love.


Your love is silent.

Mine is loud.


And even when my love fills the room, I still

hear yours.

I see the small ways you try.

The pauses. The presence. The moments you let me in.

I notice them. I adore them.


I don't know what taught you to guard your heart like this, and

I won't force myself in just to understand.

I'm here because a soul like yours deserves patience, safety, and real love.


So I'll stay outside the walls.

I'll sing.

I'll write.

.

I'll slip poems through the cracks.

Because you deserve to be loved gently and fully.


This Valentine's, I'd love to be with you.

It doesn't have to be exactly the 14th.

If Manila isn't easy, we'll make Pampanga or Tarlac ours.

Time and distance don't scare me when it comes to you.

Now let me ask,
❤️ Will you do me the honor of letting me be your Valentine?❤️
